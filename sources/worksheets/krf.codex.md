## D. Extraction notes
- Location of the party's main tax/duty table(s): page 18; related tax proposals are on page 17. Page 18 states «påløpt lettelse på om lag 2 mrd. kroner», but does not allocate it.
- Ambiguities: Page 3 reports «Skatter og avgifter -3124,0» mill. kr, while page 18 reports an accrued relief of about 2 mrd. kroner; neither total is allocated to measures. «Budsjettet i tall» starts on page 34, but pages 35–46 contain only page numbers in this text extraction.
- **[L10a] 2026-09-26 correction:** the earlier statement that the numeric annex pp. 35–46 holds the tax lines is wrong. The images show that p. 19 is KrF’s complete «Skatter og avgifter» table (bokført/påløpt, mill. kr; SUM Skatt og avgift 3124,0 / -1832,0). That resolves the p. 3 vs p. 18 ambiguity: -3124,0 is the bokført total and «om lag 2 mrd.» is the påløpt total. pp. 35–46 are spending tables (rammeområde 1–20) plus «Inntektsøkninger» (21,22). None of these pages states a tax rate. See `krf.vision.md`.

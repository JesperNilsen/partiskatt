## Findings (Codex, verbatim; verification notes in *italics*)

1. **P1 | econ | `src/views/CalculatorView.tsx:81`** — Pensioners cannot enter pension income at all. The engine has `pensionIncome`, pension minstefradrag and pension trygdeavgift, but the UI only asks for wage income. Users on pension will enter pension as wage or leave it blank, giving wrong trygdeavgift, wrong minstefradrag and wrong deltas for parties changing personfradrag/trinnskatt. Fix: add pension income per adult, plus age/pensioner status where needed.
   *Confirmed. No `pensionIncome` field anywhere in `src/views` or `src/components`; the engine and `isProfileEmpty` both support it. Seeded (prompt listed pensioners as an edge case). Note: PROJECT.md never mentions pensjon, so this is a scope gap in the brief, not a regression. Combined with item 5 it means a pensioner without taxable wealth cannot get a result at all.*

2. **P1 | econ/code | `src/engine/income-tax.ts:92`** — Pension tax credit is missing. Norwegian pension tax uses særskilt skattefradrag for pensjonsinntekt; this model taxes pensions with ordinary deductions/rates only. That does not cancel out when party rules alter personfradrag, trinnskatt, trygdeavgift or pension-relevant assumptions. Fix: add `income.pensionTaxCredit` with phase-out rules and test low/mid/high pension fixtures against Skatteetaten.
   *Confirmed. No credit in the engine; the doc comment at `income-tax.ts:80-84` even cites sktl. § 16-1 as the model for the H jobbfradrag cap. Seeded. Moot until item 1 lands; do them together.*

3. **P1 | econ/code | `src/engine/calculate-scenario.ts:92`** — Every two-adult "household" gets married-couple wealth-tax thresholds. The UI says `Husholdning`, not `ektefeller`; cohabitants and roommates are normally assessed separately for wealth tax. This can halve wealth tax for unmarried households and distort party deltas from formuesskatt. Fix: ask marital/assessment status and ownership split; apply couple thresholds only for spouses.
   *Confirmed as a modelling choice: `wealth-tax.ts:10-11` says "Couples are assessed jointly with the couple allowance and threshold" and `calculate-scenario.ts:92` passes `adults.length === 2 ? 2 : 1`. Seeded. Severity disputed: for cohabitants who hold wealth evenly (joint home) the result is identical; the bias appears only with uneven ownership, where the model understates their tax. P2 in the orchestrator's view. Cheapest fix: a "gift/registrert partner" checkbox that defaults to on, and separate thresholds otherwise with wealth split evenly.*

4. **P1 | code/econ | `src/engine/consumption.ts:28` and `:42-45`** — VAT on excise is double-counted for categories with excise duties. `netOfGross(spend, refRate)` includes the excise base, then VAT is charged on that whole base, while lines 42-45 add VAT on the excise again. VAT-rate changes on fuel/electricity/alcohol/tobacco would be overstated. Fix: split `spend` into pre-excise base and reference excise, or remove VAT-on-excise from the excise component when the VAT component already includes it.
   *Confirmed by reading the arithmetic. Spend entered incl. VAT means `netBase = base + excise`; the VAT line taxes that, and the excise line adds `excise × vat` again. Levels double-count `excise × vat`; deltas double-count `excise × Δvat` whenever a party changes the VAT rate on a category that carries excise. Pure excise changes are computed correctly. The header comment at `:13-14` claims the opposite, so the test suite is missing an identity check (total VAT = Σ netBase × rate, no second term). Independent find. Magnitude bounded to VAT-rate proposals on excise categories; check which parties have any.*

5. **P1 | ux/code | `src/state/profile.ts:126`** — The app refuses to calculate for users with no income or wealth, even though the headline includes consumption taxes and child benefits. A student, unemployed parent, retiree with no taxable wealth, or anyone wanting only VAT/excise effects is blocked by `profileEmpty`. Fix: allow calculation once the user has accepted/defaulted consumption or entered children/student data; do not require income/wealth.
   *Confirmed: `isProfileEmpty` returns true when no adult has income and no wealth field is set; children and consumption do not count. Seeded (zero income was listed). Severity fair given item 1.*

6. **P1 | econ/ux | `src/views/MethodView.tsx:124-130`** — Annualizing mid-year rules is the wrong public headline for "2026 budgets". Sp food VAT from September and child-benefit changes from February/May are shown as full-year effects, materially biasing party rankings. This is documented, but documentation does not fix the number. Fix: prorate by effective date in the default headline; optionally show "annualized policy effect" as a secondary view.
   *Location confirmed. **Challenges decision** of 2026-09-25 (full-year policy rates, documented). The strongest case is Sp's food VAT: a 1 September start means the 2026 effect is one third of the annualised figure. Middle path if the decision stands: keep the annualised headline, add a per-rule "i 2026: x kr" line derived from `effectiveDate` (L9 already carries the date).*

7. **P2 | ux/code | `src/views/CalculatorView.tsx:101`** — Adult 2 is barely modeled in the UI. The second adult only gets wage income; no pension, capital income, interest expenses, union fee or student support controls. Household results are therefore wrong for normal couples. Fix: use a repeated adult section with the same fields for both adults.
   *Confirmed; already listed as a leftover in IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN § Neste økt 6. Independent.*

8. **P2 | ux/code | `src/views/CalculatorView.tsx:223`** — Union fee is in the engine but absent from the UI. FrP's removal of fagforeningsfradrag will show zero effect for almost every user because `unionFee` is never collected. Fix: add annual union fee per adult, default 0, with the adopted cap shown.
   *Confirmed: `unionFee` exists in `profile.ts:21` and `income-tax.ts:97`, never in a view. Independent. A silent hole: the FrP rule is encoded, tested, and always yields 0 in production.*

9. **P2 | econ | `src/views/MethodView.tsx:76`** — "Kun nasjonale regler — ingen kommunal … skatt" is false as written. The engine uses municipal/fylkes income-tax rates inside the 22 percent ordinary-income tax and municipal wealth tax inside the 1.0/1.1 percent wealth-tax rates. It also assumes max/default rates and ignores geographic variants. Fix: rewrite the claim and either ask municipality/tax zone or label the calculation as standard/max-rate Norway.
   *Location confirmed. Severity disputed: the sentence actually says "ingen kommunal eiendomsskatt", which is true, and PROJECT.md:36 forbids a municipal model. The valid residue is copy: say that the 22 % and the formuesskatt rate include the municipal and county shares at standard rates (Bø and the Finnmark/Nord-Troms zone differ). P3 copy fix. Independent.*

10. **P2 | econ/ux | `src/views/ResultsView.tsx:70-73`** — Ranking all parties as comparable is misleading when large proposal bundles are excluded. This is a known decision, but it is still wrong for publication: parties with many unquantified transfers/fees/services are ranked by the quantifiable residue. Fix: rank only within "comparable core", add a coverage penalty/banner, or suppress ordinal ranking when excluded proveny/rules dominate.
    *Location confirmed ("Alle ni partier … Sortert etter mest penger igjen"). **Challenges decision** (unquantified excluded, documented). `rule-provenance.ts:90-110` already surfaces unquantified proposals per card, so the missing piece is a list-level coverage signal, e.g. "n forslag ikke tallfestet" beside each rank. Seeded (comparability was an axis).*

11. **P2 | econ | `src/data/consumption-profiles.ts:88`** — Using 2022 kroner unchanged materially understates 2026 indirect-tax effects. That especially biases parties with VAT/excise changes. This is defensible for source discipline, but not for a kroner-per-year voter headline. Fix: CPI-uplift spend categories to 2026 with a visible "2022 fixed prices" alternative.
    *Location confirmed (the 2022-kroner doc comment). **Challenges decision** recorded in METHODOLOGY.md:51. Effect is a roughly uniform ~15 % understatement of every party's VAT/excise delta, so it shifts magnitude more than ranking. Seeded but listed as known; Codex argues the decision, which was asked for.*

12. **P2 | econ/code | `src/views/CalculatorView.tsx:93-99`** — Student support defaults to 10 months and cannot be edited. Lånekassen support is normally modeled over the support months for the study year, and party proposals often state annual/monthly effects. This undercounts student-support deltas and makes R/SV/V student proposals fragile. Fix: expose `studyMonths`, default to the official support-month count, and test party deltas at 0/default/max months.
    *Confirmed: `:96` hardcodes `studyMonths: 10` on the checkbox. Independent.*

13. **P2 | build | `src/tests/party-data.test.ts:27-29`** — Tests require archived text files through `entry.textFile`. Public-release docs still say the `sources/raw`/`sources/text` rights decision is open, while Netlify runs `npm run check`. If those files are withheld or scrubbed, deploy breaks. Fix: move anchor fixtures into licensed/sanitized test data or make provenance text tests conditional in public builds.
    *Confirmed: 39 files tracked under `sources/text`, not ignored; `netlify.toml:2` runs `npm run check`, which runs vitest. If the rights call (docs/rights.md) removes the party text extractions, the public build fails. Independent, and directly relevant to gate 4.*

14. **P3 | ux/a11y | `src/views/CalculatorView.tsx:64-79`** — The segmented control has no selected-state semantics. Visual class changes are not enough for screen readers. Fix: use radio buttons or add `aria-pressed`/`aria-selected` with correct roles.
    *Confirmed: two `<button>`s toggling a class inside `role="group"`, no `aria-pressed`. Seeded (accessibility axis).*

15. **P3 | ux/a11y | `src/components/MoneyInput.tsx:56`** — Field hints are not announced. `Field` renders `${id}-hint`, but `MoneyInput` only describes the suffix. Users miss hints like "Før skatt, pensjon og trygd." Fix: pass `aria-describedby` through `Field`/input components and concatenate hint + suffix ids.
    *Confirmed: `Field.tsx:13` emits `${id}-hint`; `MoneyInput.tsx:56` sets `aria-describedby` to the suffix only. Seeded.*
